A daily declaration

My Consecration Confession

Read this slowly and sincerely. Let these words become more than a moment—let them shape a life continually surrendered to God.

Today, I recommit my life to God.

I belong to Him—my heart, my mind, my body, my time, my gifts, and my desires.

I choose obedience over convenience, purity over compromise, and God’s presence over temporary pleasures.

I will guard what enters my heart, watch the words that leave my mouth, and honor God through my choices.

I am not being consecrated to appear perfect. I am being set apart for God’s purpose.

When distractions arise, I will return my attention to Him. When temptation comes, I will remember who I belong to. When I fall short, I will not run away in shame; I will return to God with an honest and surrendered heart.

My private life will reflect my public confession. My worship will go beyond songs. My consecration will continue beyond this month.

I release every habit, relationship, desire, and distraction that weakens my devotion to God.

My ears are open to His voice.
My heart is available to His leading.
My hands are ready for His work.
My life is surrendered to His will.

I am set apart, but I am not alone. God is shaping me, strengthening me, and preparing me for His purpose.

This month may be ending, but my devotion is not. I will live daily as one who belongs completely to God.

I am His.
I am available.
I am consecrated.
